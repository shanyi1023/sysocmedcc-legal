Privacy Policy

SYsocmedCC reads your TikTok profile information (name, avatar, follower and like counts) and your video list with view, like, comment and share counts, only after you authorize it, to show analytics to you.

Data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to third parties. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored token. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
